# ILSA WEB - Product Documentation

## Multimedia Special Reports
ILSA's multimedia reports focus on high-impact visual storytelling. These reports are designed to provide a multi-layered experience for the reader, combining traditional journalism with rich media.
- **Visuals**: High-resolution imagery using `SmartImage.astro` for efficient loading and responsiveness.
- **Interactivity**: Interactive elements such as sliders, image galleries with hover effects, and complex animations via Three.js.
- **Consistency**: Each multimedia piece maintains the "Liquid Glass" aesthetic to ensure coherence with the main news feed.

## Target Audience
- **General Population**: Readers seeking high-quality, reliable news.
- **Opinion & Culture Seekers**: Individuals interested in deep dives, columns, and editorial content.
- **Mobile and Desktop Users**: The experience is optimized for both high-end desktop setups (focusing on visual polish) and standard mobile devices (focusing on readability and load speed).

## Responsive Design Requirements
- **Mobile-First Approach**: Core content must be readable and accessible on small screens.
- **Adaptive Grids**: Use responsive grid systems (e.g., 1 column for mobile, 2 for tablet, 3+ for desktop).
- **Optimized Images**: Automatic handling of image sizes and resolutions to ensure performance on mobile networks.
- **Touch-Friendly Targets**: Buttons and interactive elements must have sufficient hit areas for mobile users.
